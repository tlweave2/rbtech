How it runs

Water and power.

Water

  • Closed loop with air-cooled chillers. The same water recirculates.
  • One-time fill from existing on-site wells. The water is recycled and non-drinkable.
  • No City of Riverbank drinking water, and no agricultural water.
  • The fill is about what a few homes use in a year.

Power

  • Sourced from outside the Riverbank area, including renewable hydroelectric.
  • Uses grid and transmission capacity built for the site's former heavy industry.
  • RTC pays for any on-site upgrades. Ratepayers and taxpayers don't.
  • No gas turbines or diesel generators running day to day.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Didn't the city pause data center projects?

Yes. In September 2026 the City Council adopted a temporary moratorium to study data center proposals. We support a careful review and have published our commitments so the Council and residents can judge this project on its specifics.

How much water will it use, and where does it come from?

The closed cooling loop gets a one-time fill of recycled, non-drinkable water from existing on-site wells that aren't connected to Riverbank's municipal system. After that the water recirculates. There's no continuous draw for cooling.

Will my electricity rates go up?

RTC uses transmission capacity already built for the site's former heavy industry and pays for any on-site upgrades it needs. It doesn't ask ratepayers or taxpayers to fund new capacity.

How loud will it be?

All computing equipment is indoors, more than 200 ft from the street. The chillers are low-noise, and no turbines or generators run day to day. Noise stays below the levels already allowed in this industrial zone.

What is a "Sustainable Computing Facility"?

Our name for a computing facility that meets five standards: no continuous cooling water, power from existing grid capacity, reuse of existing buildings, industrial rather than agricultural land, and recurring local tax revenue.

Why Riverbank?

The complex already has large industrial buildings, industrial zoning, and underused grid connections. Using them avoids building on farmland or land that could be used for housing.

Who are Aemetis and Spartan?

Aemetis produces renewable fuels and low-carbon energy and has been master developer of the Riverbank Industrial Complex since 2022. Spartan designs and delivers modular computing facilities.
