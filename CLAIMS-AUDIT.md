## Claims removed from the earlier drafts (not on the original)
- The City Council moratorium, the "Under City Council review" status bar, and the "Where things stand" timeline (community meetings, environmental review, permits, annual reports)
- Every "We promise" / "How to check" / "Proof" line under the commitments (interconnection agreements on file, metered water published yearly, noise readings published, site plan on file, named contact, posted Q&A, annual reports). The original lists only the commitment titles.
- The comparison table against "typical hyperscale campuses" (hundreds of megawatts, millions of gallons, diesel generator banks)
- "Diesel" generators, "1940s" buildings, "sawtooth" roofs, "former Army ammunition plant" (the original says "U.S. Army ammunition casings production")
- "Wells aren't connected to the city system", "the same water recirculates", "underused grid connections"
- "The assumptions behind these estimates are in the PDF" and "sources for every number"
- "A real person reads every message", "we'll add the answer", the updates mailing list
- Photo captions such as "Life jacket donations" and "Bikes for local kids". Captions are now the original titles, and alt text describes only what is visible.
- "All equipment indoors" (the original says *computing* equipment is indoors and *all* equipment is 200+ ft from the street)

## Illustrations
The setback and cooling-loop drawings are labeled "Simplified illustration, not to scale" and, where they show a layout, "not the actual site layout". Where things sit in them (for example, chillers drawn beside the building) is illustrative only.

## Changed at the client's request (preview C, the selected design)
- All contact goes to info@riverbanktc.com (the address on the RTC social media flyer) instead of Community@aemetis.com.
- Preview C now has a contact form and a "Get project updates" email sign-up. Both send to info@riverbanktc.com.

## Removed at the client's request
- The dollar figures for property tax ($10M+/yr, and the $5M+/yr for schools that is described as part of it). The previews now say "increased property tax revenue" with no amount.

## Left out on purpose
The original also says "can supply excess power back to the grid", "potential future power produced onsite", and "equivalent to the annual use of two homes". The previews leave these out. Omitting a claim can't add one, and "two homes" conflicts with "a few homes" elsewhere on the original.
